This document is providing an overview of the 4 weeks (5 with prep week) training and what they will cover for SAP CI:

Week 1 - Online training (self-paced) -> https://learning.sap.com/courses/sap-cloud-platform-integration-service-overview

Following weeks will include a short intro into the targets for the week, expectations and expected results.
Each week is with duration of 5 days and would have both practical and theoretical knowledge share.
After each week below, you will be contacted separetely in pre-defined slots of the following week, where short quizes and questions will help you structure your knowledge better and be prepared for the final exam.

To avoid constant exchange of messages between the trainer and the trainees, you will keep a training journal in Teams under your folder name. The purpose of the Journal is to write in your own words the things you understood and learned from the day and those of which you feel unsure about or completely don't understand. The trainer will check those Journals at occasion and will update some answers on the spot, others will be kept for the personal review of the week.

Week 2 - Foundations: Platform, iFlows, Adapters
Week 3 - Mappings, Groovy v2 & Scripting Conventions
Week 4 - Security, Async Messaging & Reusability
Week 5 - Monitoring, Transports, Event-Driven & Error Handling

Each practical tasks MUST adhere to strict naming conventions to avoid overlapping/overwriting the content of other trainees. In case the instructions miss to mention this - ALWAYS prefix your own content with your own two letter code, which you can also find in the folder structure of the training.
Keep important information about your practical tasks in your Journal - e.g. configurations, endpoints, special conditions, questions, topics you don't know why you are doing, etc.

After the training is complete, there will be some time - between 1-2 weeks left for the participants to structure their knowledge, reflect on what they have learned and refine (if needed) their practical tasks.

Following this period - a personalized exam (oral) will be conducted with each participant ranging from theoretical questions on the material covered to brainstorming on concrete customer challenges and how those can be resolved with the gained knowledge.
